Privacy Policy

Romdix SMS Connector — last updated 17 November 2026

Romdix SMS Connector ("the App") connects a CRM sub-account to an SMS provider so that messages can be sent, delivery status can be reported, and replies can be shown inside the CRM. This policy explains what we store and why.

Data we collect

  • The sub-account (location) identifier provided by the CRM platform when the App is installed.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the CRM platform for that sub-account.
  • SMS provider credentials you enter: username/password or API key/secret and sender ID.
  • Message metadata needed for delivery tracking: message identifiers, recipient number and delivery status.
  • Inbound message content, when your SMS provider supports two-way messaging and it is enabled on your account.

Why we collect it

  • To authenticate your settings page inside the CRM.
  • To send messages through your own SMS provider account.
  • To update message delivery status and display replies in your CRM conversations.

How it is stored and protected

Data is stored in a private database on our server. Provider credentials and platform tokens are encrypted at rest, and all traffic uses HTTPS. Secrets are never sent back to the browser after they are saved.

Sharing

We do not sell data. Data is shared only with the services required to operate the App: your SMS provider (to deliver messages and retrieve status/replies) and the CRM platform's API (to report status and add inbound messages).

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the App is installed. Using the App's Disconnect action, or uninstalling the App, deletes the stored credentials and tokens for that sub-account. You can also request deletion by email.

Cookies and tracking

The App does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. A short-lived session token is kept in memory in your browser to authorise settings requests.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@romdix.com
