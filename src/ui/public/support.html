Support

Romdix SMS Connector

Email: support@romdix.com

Response time: within 1 business day (Sunday–Thursday, Bangladesh time).

Before you write

  • Check that your SMS provider credentials (API key/secret or username/password and sender ID) are saved in the app.
  • Use Test Connection in the app to confirm the provider accepts your credentials.
  • For two-way replies with Icombd, ask Icombd to enable the SMS Inbox feature on your account.
  • If messages are rejected, confirm your sender ID is approved by your provider.

When you contact us, include

  • Your sub-account (location) ID.
  • The approximate time of the problem.
  • The error message shown in the app (do not send your API keys or passwords).
