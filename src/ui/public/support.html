Romdix SMS Connector

Email: support@romdix.com

Response time: within 1 business day (Sunday–Thursday, Bangladesh time).

Before you write

  • Check that your Zender Site URL and Zender API Key are saved and active in the app.
  • Use Test Connection in the app to confirm your Zender server accepts your API key.
  • For two-way replies and live delivery reports, confirm your Zender webhook is subscribed to sms.received, sms.sent, sms.delivered, and sms.failed.
  • If messages stay queued in Zender, check your Zender device status or cron scheduler.

When you contact us, include

  • Your sub-account (location) ID.
  • The approximate time of the problem.
  • The error message shown in the app (do not send your API keys or passwords).
